Pregnancy · 15 min
Golden milk oats.

1 cup rolled oats · 2 cups milk · ½ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp cinnamon · ¼ tsp ginger · a pinch of black pepper · honey · banana.

Warm the milk with the spices until golden. Stir in the oats. Five minutes on low. That’s breakfast.

From Bella

That pinch of pepper isn’t decoration. It helps your body take in the turmeric.
