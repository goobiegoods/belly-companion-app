Day 01 of 30 · The Landing

You just did the hardest thing.

If you read nothing else

Feed. Sleep. Be held. That’s the whole list.

For you
01
Heavy, bright red bleeding is normal today. It’s called lochia.
02
Pee early and often. Peri bottle with warm water, every time.
03
Eat something warm within the hour. Not a granola bar. Warm.
For baby
01
Colostrum is tiny and it’s enough. Baby’s stomach is about the size of a cherry.
02
Expect about 1 wet diaper and black, tar-like poop (meconium).
03
Skin-to-skin as much as you can, with daddy too. It steadies baby’s heart rate, breathing and temperature.
Comic · 60 seconds

How to change a diaper

01Clean diaper open, under baby, before the old one comes off.
02Wipe front to back. Pat dry. Air for a few seconds.
03Front up, fold it below the cord stump.
04Tabs snug, two fingers fit at the waist. Done, hero.
The warm bowl · TCM
Ginger + red date tea

3 slices fresh ginger, 4 red dates, 2 cups water. Simmer 15 min, sip all day. In Chinese tradition, sitting the month starts with warmth. No ice water.

Day 02 of 30 · The Landing

The second night is famous.

If you read nothing else

Baby may want to feed all night. That’s the plan, not a problem.

For you
01
Cramping during feeds is your uterus shrinking back. Annoying. Healthy.
02
A warm (not hot) heat pack on your lower belly helps.
03
Say yes to every offer of help today. All of them.
For baby
01
Feed on early cues: rooting, sucking hands, turning toward you. Crying is a late cue.
02
About 2 wet diapers today.
03
Cluster feeding tonight tells your body to make more milk. Baby is placing an order.
Comic · Night two

Baby is placing an order

01Cluster feeding isn’t a problem. It’s a purchase order for tomorrow’s milk.
02Your body reads every feed and makes more. Say yes to the order.
The warm bowl · TCM
Plain congee

Rice porridge, cooked low and long. In TCM it’s the gentlest food for a depleted digestion. Add ginger, scallion, a pinch of salt.

Day 03 of 30 · The Landing

Milk, tears, and both at once.

If you read nothing else

Crying for no reason today is hormones. Not failure.

For you
01
Milk often comes in between days 2 and 5. Full, hot, heavy, sometimes rock-hard.
02
Feed often. Cool cabbage leaves between feeds for swelling.
03
Baby blues are real: weepy, raw, overwhelmed. They usually peak days 3 to 5.
For baby
01
Poop is turning green-brown. Good sign. Milk is moving through.
02
First pediatrician visit is usually around now (day 3 to 5). What to expect: a weigh-in, a feeding check, a look at skin color. Bring your questions.
03
Look at baby’s skin in daylight. A little yellow on the face is common.
04
Baby can cry. Sometimes it’s just adjusting to the real world.
The warm bowl · TCM
Bone broth with ginger

Sip a mug before each long feed. Warm fluid in, milk out. Traditional in almost every culture that keeps a recovery month.

Worth knowing
Why newborns cry

Your baby just left the warmest, quietest, darkest place they will ever know. Light, air, hunger and noise are all brand new. Crying is how they adjust. Hold, sway, shush. You’re their translator now.
