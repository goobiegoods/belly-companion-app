# Belly Automatic Renewal Terms

**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026

These Automatic Renewal Terms are part of the [Belly Terms of Service](/terms). They explain how Belly membership billing works.

---

# 1. What You're Signing Up For

Belly membership is a subscription that **renews automatically** until you cancel.

Before you confirm, we show you:

* the price and how often you'll be charged (for example, $89 per year);
* the length of any free trial;
* when your first charge will happen;
* how to cancel.

Payments are processed by Stripe. You'll see these terms again on the secure Stripe checkout page before you confirm.

---

# 2. Free Trials

* You won't be charged during your free trial.
* A payment method is required to start a trial.
* **If you don't cancel before your trial ends, your membership starts automatically and you'll be charged the price shown at signup.**
* Free trials are limited to one per person.

---

# 3. Renewal

Your membership renews automatically at the end of each billing period, and your payment method is charged the price for your plan at that time, until you cancel.

If we ever change the price of your plan, we'll tell you in advance by email, and the new price will apply only from your next renewal after that notice.

---

# 4. How to Cancel

You can cancel at any time, online, in a few taps:

* **In the app:** Me → Manage → Cancel.
* **By email:** write to **hello@meetbelly.com** from the email on your account.

If you cancel during a free trial, you won't be charged. If you cancel after a charge, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

---

# 5. Refunds

Charges are generally non-refundable except where required by law or as described in our [Terms of Service](/terms). If you think you were charged in error, contact hello@meetbelly.com.

---

# 6. Contact Us

Questions about billing? Email **hello@meetbelly.com**.
