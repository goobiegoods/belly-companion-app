# Belly angles: organic first, then retargeting

Source: what moms actually say on r/pregnant and r/BabyBumps.
Top "nobody told me" themes, in order: 1st-trimester cramping/spotting fear · nausea + hunger at once ·
bone-deep exhaustion · doctors brushing them off · pelvic/rib/sciatica pain · mood swings + isolation ·
peeing constantly · skin/hair changes · can't sleep · kick-count confusion.
What they love in apps: accurate, calm, no ads, not condescending, partner features, size comparisons.

## 10 organic hooks (reels or carousels, 15-30s, face to camera)
1. "Things nobody tells you about the first trimester" (list; the comments will be full of leads)
2. "Being starving AND nauseous at the same time should be illegal"
3. "POV: it's 3am and you're googling a cramp" -> show calm Bella answer (no scary forums)
4. "My OB gave me 7 minutes. Here's the list I bring now" (free checklist = lead magnet)
5. "Pregnancy tired is not normal tired" (relatable, gets shared)
6. "Rate these pregnancy pains 1-10: round ligament, rib, sciatica, heartburn"
7. "Things your partner can do this week that actually help" (tag your partner = reach)
8. "Baby is the size of a ___ this week and I'm the size of a ___" (weekly series)
9. "Comment your due month and meet your bump buddies" (proven, our best engagement)
10. "What I wish I'd asked at my first appointment" (comment BELLY = we send the list)

Rules: hook in first 1s, captions on screen, post 9am-7pm PT, reels over images,
1 CTA only: "comment BELLY" or "link in bio". Keep reposting memes 3:1 with these.

## Retargeting (audiences built in Ads Manager > Belly Ads)
- IG engagers 365d · FB page engagers 365d · Site visitors 180d

- 2 ads to test:
  A) "The 3am questions, answered kindly. Bella is a 24/7 virtual doula. Free for 7 days."
