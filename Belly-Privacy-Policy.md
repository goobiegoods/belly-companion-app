# Belly Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

Welcome to Belly.

Your privacy is important to us. We built Belly to support mothers through educational wellness resources, and we believe your personal information should be handled with care, transparency, and respect.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By using Belly, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

# 1. Who We Are

Belly is a consumer wellness platform that provides educational resources for pregnancy, postpartum, motherhood, nutrition, herbal wellness, homeopathy, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), ingredient education, and related wellness topics.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

**Email:** hello@meetbelly.com

**Website:** https://meetbelly.com

---

# 2. Information We Collect

## Information You Provide

When you create an account or use Belly, you may provide information such as:

* Your name
* Email address
* Password (stored securely through our authentication provider)
* Due date
* Children's ages
* Wellness interests or preferences
* Profile information you choose to add
* Messages you send to Bella
* Information you voluntarily submit to our community
* Information you send when contacting support

You may choose not to provide certain information, but some features may not function properly without it.

---

## Bella Conversations

When you chat with Bella, we collect:

* Questions you ask
* Bella's responses
* Conversation history

We store your conversations so we can provide your chat history, improve your experience, troubleshoot issues, maintain service quality, and continue conversations across devices.

---

## Ingredient Scanner

If you use the ingredient scanner, we process the photos you upload in order to analyze product ingredients and provide educational information.

Unless otherwise stated within the app, uploaded scanner images are not retained longer than reasonably necessary to complete the requested analysis.

---

## Community Content

If you participate in Belly's community features, we collect the content you voluntarily submit, including posts, comments, replies, and other interactions.

Information you choose to post publicly may be visible to other users.

Please avoid sharing information you would not want others to see.

---

## Purchases

If you purchase products or subscriptions through Belly, payment information is securely processed by Stripe.

Belly does **not** receive or store your complete payment card information.

We may receive limited transaction information such as:

* purchase date
* subscription status
* product purchased
* transaction identifiers
* payment confirmation

---

## Usage Information

When you use Belly, we may collect information about how the app is used, including:

* pages or screens viewed
* features used
* ingredient scans performed
* course progress
* notification interactions
* subscription activity
* general app performance
* device type
* operating system
* app version

We use this information to improve Belly, understand how features are used, identify bugs, and enhance the user experience.

We do not intentionally use analytics to collect the content of your private conversations with Bella.
